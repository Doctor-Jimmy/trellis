\begin{lemma}[Multi-sequence perfectness gives super-sequence perfectness]
If $h$ is perfect and $h^{\dcl}$ is its induced value map on the deciding
front, then $h^{\dcl}$ is perfect as a super-sequence.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Given $s\mathrel{\mathsf{FiniteShift}}t$, choose an enumeration $x$ which
witnesses the shift.  The coherence equation identifies the two
super-sequence values with $h(x)$ and $h(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x))$.  Apply
perfectness of $h$ and use \noderef{FiniteShift} and
\noderef{PerfectSuperSequence}.
\end{proof}
