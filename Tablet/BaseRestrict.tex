\begin{definition}
Let $h:\mathsf{BaseIncSeq}(X)\to E$ and let
$z\in\mathsf{BaseIncSeq}(X)$.  The restriction of $h$ to the infinite
subbase enumerated by $z$ is represented in global coordinates by the
multi-sequence
\[
h\mathbin{|}z:\mathsf{IncSeq}\to E,\qquad
(h\mathbin{|}z)(x)=h(z\circ x).
\]
The range of $z\circ x$ is contained in $X$, so the right-hand side is
defined.  This is the increasing-enumeration form of passing to the
infinite subbase enumerated by $z$; it deliberately differs from the
right-composition operation $x\mapsto x\circ z$.
\end{definition}
