\begin{theorem}[MainProp]
For every increasing injective function $g:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ with
$g\neq\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb N}$, the dynamical systems of increasing
embeddings given by right composition with $g$ and by the ordinary shift
are continuously homomorphically equivalent:
\[
(\mathsf{IncSeq},\mathsf{RightComp}(g))
\quad\text{and}\quad
(\mathsf{IncSeq},\mathsf{ShiftMap}).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The node \noderef{RmapLemma} supplies a prefix-continuous homomorphism from
right composition by $g$ to the shift.  The node \noderef{EmapLemma} supplies
a prefix-continuous homomorphism in the reverse direction.  These are exactly
the two conjuncts in the definition of continuous homomorphic equivalence,
so the displayed systems are continuously homomorphically equivalent.
\end{proof}
