\begin{lemma}[Atoms are hereditarily countable]
For every $q\in Q$, the tagged atom $\mathsf{atom}(q)$ is hereditarily
countable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the one-atom code whose code is $\mathsf{atom}(q)$.  By the defining
equation of $\noderef{HerCtblCodeErase}$, its erasure is the same tagged
atom.  Reflexivity of $\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}$, supplied by
$\noderef{PowerPresentationEqEquivalence}$, is therefore the witness in
the definition of $\noderef{HereditarilyCountable}$.
\end{proof}
