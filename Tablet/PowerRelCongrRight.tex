\begin{lemma}[Right presentation invariance]
If $\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}(Y,Y')$, then for every
$X\in\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$,
\[
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(X,Y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(X,Y').
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The proof is the right-hand analogue of left invariance.  Induct on the
well-founded child descent of the right presentations and unfold
\noderef{PowerRel}.  Equal atom presentations give equal $Q$-values by
\noderef{PowerPresentationEq}; mixed tags are impossible.  For two right
nodes, the two matching conjuncts in \noderef{PowerPresentationEq} transport
the existential right-child witnesses in the atom--node and node--node
reductions \noderef{PowerRelAtomNode} and \noderef{PowerRelNodeNode}.  For a
left node and right atom, the atom equality case is immediate from
\noderef{PowerRelNodeAtom}.  Apply the induction hypothesis to each matched
child comparison and use the reverse matching conjunct for the converse
direction.  The fact that matching witnesses can be reversed and composed
is the equivalence property in \noderef{PowerPresentationEqEquivalence}.
\end{proof}
