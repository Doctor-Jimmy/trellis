\begin{lemma}[Well-foundedness of copied pair descent]
The relation $\mathsf{PowerGameDescent}$ on pairs of
$\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$-presentations is well-founded.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The relation in \noderef{PowerGameDescent} is the lexicographic product of
two copies of the child relation.  The child relation is well-founded by
\noderef{PowerChildWellFounded}.  The standard lexicographic-product
construction therefore supplies a well-founded relation on pairs, which is
exactly the displayed relation.
\end{proof}
