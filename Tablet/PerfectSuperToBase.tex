\begin{lemma}[Super-sequence perfectness gives base perfectness]
If $f:F\to Q$ is perfect, then the locally constant extension
$f^{\upcl}$ on $X$ is perfect as a base multi-sequence.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use \noderef{SuperSequenceExtension} to obtain the extension.  For an
enumeration $x$ of a subset of $X$, the front members selected for $x$ and
its shift form a finite shift.  Perfectness of $f$ therefore gives the
relation between the two extension values.  This is precisely
\noderef{BasePerfect}.
\end{proof}
