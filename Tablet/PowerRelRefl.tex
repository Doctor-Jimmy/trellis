\begin{lemma}[Reflexivity of the lifted relation]
If $r$ is a preorder on $Q$, then
\[
\forall X\in\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q),\quad
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(X,X).
\].
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Proceed by well-founded induction on the child relation from
\noderef{PowerChildWellFounded}.  For an atom $\mathsf{atom}(q)$, the
atom--atom reduction \noderef{PowerRelAtomAtom} reduces the goal to
$r(q,q)$, which is reflexivity of the assumed preorder on $Q$.  For a node
$X=\mathsf{node}(\iota,h,f)$, use the node--node reduction
\noderef{PowerRelNodeNode}.  Given $i\in\iota$, choose the same index $i$ on
the right; the induction hypothesis applied to the child $f(i)$ proves
$\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(f(i),f(i))$.  This supplies the required witness for
every child, and hence proves reflexivity for $X$.
\end{proof}
