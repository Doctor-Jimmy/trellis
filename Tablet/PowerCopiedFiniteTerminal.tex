\begin{lemma}[Finite terminal dependence]
If $h$ is bad for $\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$, then for every $x$ there are
$k\in\mathbb N$ and atoms $q,q'\in Q$ such that
\[
L_{0,k}=\mathsf{atom}(q),\qquad L_{1,k}=\mathsf{atom}(q'),
\]
and the atom $q$ is unchanged by replacing $h$ with any multi-sequence that
agrees with $h$ on the finite shift prefix
$h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(j,x))$ for $j\le k+2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{PowerCopiedTerminates} to row zero and choose a witnessing
depth and the two atom labels.  The finite-dependence statement
\noderef{PowerCopiedFiniteDependence}, with $n=0$, says that the first
terminal value is unchanged under every replacement agreeing through shift
$k+2$.  Since the original value is $\mathsf{atom}(q)$, the replacement has
the same form and label.  This proves all three displayed assertions.
\end{proof}
