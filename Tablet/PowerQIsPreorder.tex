\begin{lemma}[The lifted relation is a preorder]
If $r$ is a preorder on $Q$, then $\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$ is a preorder on
$\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The reflexivity law is exactly \noderef{PowerRelRefl}, and the transitivity
law is exactly \noderef{PowerRelTrans}; both have the same preorder
hypothesis on $r$.  Combining these two laws gives the named
$\mathsf{IsPreorder}$ instance for $\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$ on the full tagged
hierarchy.  No equality or quotient of raw presentations is used in this
instance.
\end{proof}
