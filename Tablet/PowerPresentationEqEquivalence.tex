\begin{lemma}[Structural equivalence is an equivalence relation]
The relation $\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}$ is reflexive, symmetric, and
transitive on $\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For reflexivity, induct on a presentation.  An atom matches itself by
equality.  At a node, pair each child with itself and apply the induction
hypothesis, giving both directions of the matching condition in
\noderef{PowerPresentationEqShape}.  For symmetry, exchange the two
matching witnesses at every node; the induction hypotheses reverse the
equivalence of each matched pair, and atom equality is symmetric.  For
transitivity, compose the child matchings: a child of the first node matches
a child of the middle node, which matches a child of the last node, so the
induction hypothesis gives an equivalent child in the last node.  The reverse
direction is identical.  The atom cases compose equality, and the mixed
cases are impossible by the disjointness part of
\noderef{PowerPresentationEqShape}.  Therefore all three equivalence laws
hold.
\end{proof}
