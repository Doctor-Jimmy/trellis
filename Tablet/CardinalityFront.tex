\begin{definition}[Constant-cardinality front]
For $k\in\mathbb N$ and an infinite $X\subseteq\mathbb N$, let
\[
\mathsf{CardinalityFront}(k,X)=\{s\subseteq X\mid s\text{ is finite and }|s|=k\}.
\]
Finite subsets are represented by `Finset $\mathbb N$`, so this is the
Lean set of finite sets of cardinality $k$ contained in $X$.  These are
the fronts $[X]^k$ used for the triple and quadruple colorings.
\end{definition}
