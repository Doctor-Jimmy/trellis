\begin{lemma}[Well-founded hierarchy descent]
The child-to-parent relation
$\mathsf{PowerChild}(x,y)$ on $\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$ is well-founded.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We prove by structural induction on $y$ that $y$ is accessible for the
relation in \noderef{PowerChild}.  If $y$ is an atom, the definition of
\noderef{PowerChild} gives no $x$ with $\mathsf{PowerChild}(x,y)$, so the
accessibility constructor applies immediately.  If
$y=\mathsf{node}(\iota,h,f)$ and
$\mathsf{PowerChild}(x,y)$, then by the definition there is an index $i$
with $x=f(i)$.  The induction hypothesis supplies accessibility of $f(i)$;
the equality $x=f(i)$ transports that accessibility to $x$.  Hence every
predecessor of $y$ is accessible, and the accessibility constructor gives
accessibility of $y$.  Since $y$ was arbitrary, the relation is well-founded.
\end{proof}
