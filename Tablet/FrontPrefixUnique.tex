\begin{lemma}[Front member uniqueness]
Under the hypotheses of \noderef{FrontPrefixExists}, the member of $F$
which is an initial segment of the range of $x$ is unique.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $s$ and $t$ both satisfy \(\mathsf{FrontPrefix}(F,-,x)\), then both are
members of $F$ and both are initial segments of the same increasing set.
Consequently one is an initial segment of the other.  The prefix-free clause
of \noderef{Front} forces $s=t$.
\end{proof}
