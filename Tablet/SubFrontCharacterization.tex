\begin{lemma}[Sub-fronts]
Let $F$ be a front on $X$ and let $F'\subseteq F$.  Then there is a set $Y$
such that $F'$ is a front on $Y$ if and only if there is an infinite
$Z\subseteq X$ such that
\[
F'=F\mathbin{|}Z.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the reverse implication, suppose that $Z\subseteq X$ is infinite and
$F'=F\mathbin{|}Z$.  By \noderef{FrontRestriction}, applied to the front
$F$ on $X$, the family defined by \noderef{FrontRestrict} is a front on
$Z$.  Thus the left side holds, and the defining condition of
\noderef{FrontRestrict} also gives $F'\subseteq F$.

For the forward implication, suppose that $F'$ is a front on some $Y$ and
$F'\subseteq F$.  First consider the trivial case $F=\{\emptyset\}$.
The density clause of \noderef{Front} makes $F'$ nonempty, while the
inclusion in $F$ forces $F'=\{\emptyset\}$.  Taking $Z=X$, which is
infinite by \noderef{Front}, gives
$F'=\mathsf{FrontRestrict}(F,Z)$ by \noderef{FrontRestrict}.

Now suppose that $F\ne\{\emptyset\}$.  Then $\emptyset\notin F$: if it
were in $F$, it would be an initial segment of every member of $F$, and the
prefix-free clause of \noderef{Front} would force every member of $F$ to be
$\emptyset$.  The same observation and $F'\subseteq F$ show that
$\emptyset\notin F'$.  Hence the base clauses in \noderef{Front} give
\[
\mathsf{FrontBase}(F')=Y\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathsf{FrontBase}(F)=X.
\]
Since $F'\subseteq F$, the definition of \noderef{FrontBase} gives
$Y\subseteq X$.  Put $Z=Y$; it is infinite by the infinite-base clause of
\noderef{Front}.  It remains to show $F'=F\mathbin{|}Y$.

If $s\in F'$, every element of $s$ belongs to
$\mathsf{FrontBase}(F')=Y$, so $s\in F\mathbin{|}Y$.  Conversely, let
$s\in F\mathbin{|}Y$.  Since $s$ is a nonempty finite initial segment and
$Y$ is infinite, the tail $Y/s$ is infinite; hence
$s\cup(Y/s)\subseteq Y$ is infinite.  The density clause of
\noderef{Front} for $F'$, applied to this set, supplies $t\in F'$ with
$t\segs(s\cup(Y/s))$.  Both $s$ and $t$ belong to $F$, and both are initial
segments of this same increasing set, so they are comparable by
\noderef{InitialSegment}.  Prefix-freeness of $F$ from \noderef{Front}
then forces $s=t$.  Thus $F\mathbin{|}Y\subseteq F'$, proving the desired
equality and the forward implication.
\end{proof}
