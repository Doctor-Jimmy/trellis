\begin{lemma}[Perfectness under restriction]
If a multi-sequence is perfect, then every sub-multi-sequence obtained by
restriction along an increasing embedding is perfect.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For an increasing enumeration $x$, evaluate the original perfectness
assertion at the fixed-left restriction $z\circ x$.  By
\noderef{MultiSequenceRestrict}, the restricted value at $x$ is
$h(z\circ x)$.  Its shifted value is $h(z\circ\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x))$,
which \noderef{RestrictionShift} identifies with
$h(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(z\circ x))$.  The original perfectness assertion
therefore gives the required relation, exactly the definition of
\noderef{PerfectMultiSequence} for $h\mathbin{|}z$.
\end{proof}
