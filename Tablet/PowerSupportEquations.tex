\begin{lemma}[Support equations]
For every $q\in Q$,
$\mathsf{PowerSupport}(\mathsf{atom}(q))=\{q\}$.  For every nonempty
indexed family $f:\iota\to\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$, the support of
\mathsf{node}$(f)$ is
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(\mathsf{node}(f))
 =\bigcup_{i\in\iota}\mathsf{PowerSupport}(f(i)).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both identities are the defining equations of the recursive support in
\noderef{PowerSupport}.  In the atom case the recursion returns the singleton
of the atom.  In the node case it returns the indexed union of the recursive
supports of the child family.  The two displayed equations therefore hold
for every choice of the carrier, index type, nonemptiness witness, and child
map.
\end{proof}
