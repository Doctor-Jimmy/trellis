\begin{definition}
Let $r$ be a binary relation on $Q$.  A multi-sequence $h$ is bad for $r$ if
for every increasing enumeration $x$ one has
\[
\neg r\bigl(h(x),h(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x))\bigr).
\]
For a quasi-order this is exactly the paper's condition
$h(N)\nleq h(\operatorname{shift}(N))$ for every infinite $N$.
\end{definition}
