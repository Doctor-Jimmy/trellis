\begin{lemma}[Finite-prefix agreement survives finitely many shifts]
If two increasing enumerations agree through their first $n+j$ entries, then
their $j$-fold shifts agree through their first $n$ entries:
\[
\mathsf{PrefixAgree}(n+j,x,y)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{PrefixAgree}\bigl(n,\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(j,x),
  \mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(j,y)\bigr).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We prove the assertion by induction on $j$, simultaneously for every
prefix length $n$.  For $j=0$, the defining equation for
\noderef{PowerShiftIter} gives
\(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(0,x)=x\) and
\(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(0,y)=y\).  Since $n+0=n$, the conclusion is
\(\mathsf{PrefixAgree}(n,x,y)\), which is exactly the hypothesis
\(h_{xy}\).

Suppose now that the assertion has been proved for $j$, for every prefix
length.  Let
\(h_{xy}:\mathsf{PrefixAgree}(n+(j+1),x,y)\).  The elementary natural-number
identity
\[
n+(j+1)=(n+1)+j
\]
therefore turns this into
\(\mathsf{PrefixAgree}((n+1)+j,x,y)\).  Apply the induction hypothesis
with prefix parameter $n+1$.  Writing
\[
a=\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(j,x),\qquad
b=\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(j,y),
\]
we obtain, by the definition of \noderef{PrefixAgree}, the pointwise
statement
\[
\forall k<n+1,\qquad a(k)=b(k).
\]

It remains to prove agreement after one further shift.  By the successor
equation in \noderef{PowerShiftIter}, the two sequences in the desired
conclusion are \(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(a)\) and \(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(b)\).
Fix an arbitrary \(i<n\).  By the definitions of \noderef{ShiftMap},
\noderef{RightComp}, and \noderef{SuccSeq},
\[
\mathsf{ShiftMap}(a)(i)=a(i+1),\qquad
\mathsf{ShiftMap}(b)(i)=b(i+1).
\]
The inequality \(i<n\) implies \(i+1<n+1\), so the preceding pointwise
statement, applied at $i+1$, gives \(a(i+1)=b(i+1)\).  Consequently
\(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(a)(i)=\mathsf{ShiftMap}(b)(i)\) for every $i<n$.
Unfolding \noderef{PrefixAgree} now yields
\(\mathsf{PrefixAgree}(n,\mathsf{ShiftMap}(a),\mathsf{ShiftMap}(b))\),
which is exactly the required successor case after unfolding
\noderef{PowerShiftIter} once more.
\end{proof}
