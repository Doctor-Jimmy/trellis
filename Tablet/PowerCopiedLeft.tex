\begin{definition}
Let $h$ be a multi-sequence with values in $\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$.  Define
$\mathsf{PowerCopiedLeft}_r(h,x,n,k)$ recursively on $k$.  At depth zero it
is the canonical Player I response to
\[
\bigl(h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x)),
h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n+1,x))\bigr).
\]
At depth $k+1$ it is the canonical response to the two values at depth $k$
in rows $n$ and $n+1$.  Thus row $n+1$ supplies exactly the copied right
move for row $n$, and the whole triangular play is a fixed function of the
raw values of $h$ on its shift orbit.
\end{definition}
