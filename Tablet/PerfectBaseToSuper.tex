\begin{lemma}[Perfect base extensions yield perfect sub-super-sequences]
Let $f:F\to Q$ be a super-sequence on $X$, and let $h$ be a locally
constant extension satisfying the value equation of
\noderef{SuperSequenceExtension}.  If a restriction of $h$ is perfect, then
$f$ has a perfect restriction to a sub-front on an infinite subbase of $X$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $x_0$ enumerate $X$ by \noderef{InfiniteSetEnumeration}, and let $Y$ be
the range of $x_0\circ z$; it is an
infinite subset of $X$.  By \noderef{FrontRestriction}, $F\mathbin{|}Y$ is
a front on $Y$.  Any two of its members related by a finite shift are the
front members selected by two consecutive base enumerations in the
restricted base multi-sequence.  The extension equation identifies their
values with the corresponding values of $\mathsf{BaseRestrict}(h,z)$.
Its perfectness therefore proves perfectness of the restricted
super-sequence, using \noderef{SuperSequenceRestrict}.
\end{proof}
