\begin{lemma}[Support respects presentation equivalence]
If two raw presentations are structurally equivalent, then they have the same
support:
\[
\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}(X,Y)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(X)=\mathsf{PowerSupport}(Y).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unfold the recursive definitions of structural matching and support from
\noderef{PowerPresentationEq} and \noderef{PowerSupport}, and induct on the
matching presentations.  In the atom case, the relation says that the two
atom values are equal, so their singleton supports are equal.  In either
mixed atom--node case, the relation reduces to False.  In the node case,
prove mutual support inclusion.  If an atom belongs to the support of the
first node, the support definition places it in the support of some child
$f(i)$.  The forward matching clause supplies $j$ with
$\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}(f(i),g(j))$; the induction hypothesis places
the atom in the support of $g(j)$, hence in the support of the second node.
The backward matching clause gives the reverse inclusion.  Extensionality of
sets now yields equality of supports.
\end{proof}
