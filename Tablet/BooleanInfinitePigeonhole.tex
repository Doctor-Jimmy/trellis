\begin{lemma}[Infinite pigeonhole]
For every coloring $b:\mathbb N\to\{0,1\}$, one of the two color classes
$\{n\mid b(n)=0\}$ and $\{n\mid b(n)=1\}$ is infinite.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The two color classes partition $\mathbb N$.  If both were finite, their
union would be finite, contradicting that $\mathbb N$ is infinite.  Hence at
least one class is infinite, which is the asserted color $c$.
\end{proof}
