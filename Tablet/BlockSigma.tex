\begin{definition}
Let $g\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$ and let $k$ be a point with $k<g(k)$.  For
$f\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$ define the block map $\sigma_f$ by
\[
\sigma_f(l)=
\begin{cases}
l,&l<G(0),\\
g^{[f(n)-n]}(l),&G(n)\leq l<G(n+1),
\end{cases}
\]
where $G(n)=g^{[n]}(k)$.  The Lean definition uses the least block index;
the coverage and uniqueness of that index are recorded separately.
\end{definition}
