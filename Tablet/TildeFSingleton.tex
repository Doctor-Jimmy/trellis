\begin{definition}
If the front $F$ is nontrivial, meaning $\emptyset\notin F$, the singleton
restriction of the recursively constructed map is the sequence
\[
 \widetilde f\!\restriction[\mathbb N]^1:\mathbb N\to\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q),
 \qquad n\longmapsto
 \widetilde f(\{n\}).
\]
The singleton-tree membership needed here is supplied by
\noderef{FrontTreeSingleton}.  By \noderef{TildeFHerCtbl}, every term of
this sequence is in the hereditary countable carrier.
\end{definition}
