\begin{lemma}
Every increasing self-embedding of $\mathbb N$ lies pointwise above the
identity: for every $f\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$ and every $n\in\mathbb N$,
$n\leq f(n)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $f$ and argue by induction on $n$.  The base case is $0\leq f(0)$.
For the successor step, assume the induction hypothesis $n\leq f(n)$.
By \noderef{IncSeq}, $f$ is an order embedding.  Since $n<n+1$, its
order-embedding property gives $f(n)<f(n+1)$, and therefore
$f(n)+1\leq f(n+1)$.  The induction hypothesis also gives
$n+1\leq f(n)+1$ by monotonicity of successor.  Transitivity now yields
$n+1\leq f(n+1)$.
\end{proof}
