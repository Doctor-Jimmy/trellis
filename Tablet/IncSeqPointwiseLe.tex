\begin{lemma}
Every increasing self-embedding of $\mathbb N$ lies pointwise above the
identity: for every $f\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$ and every $n\in\mathbb N$,
$n\leq f(n)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $f$ and argue by induction on $n$.  The base case is $0\leq f(0)$.
If $n\leq f(n)$, then strict monotonicity gives
$f(n)+1\leq f(n+1)$, while the induction hypothesis gives
$n+1\leq f(n)+1$.  Transitivity yields $n+1\leq f(n+1)$.
\end{proof}
