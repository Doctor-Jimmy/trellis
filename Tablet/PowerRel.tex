\begin{definition}
For a relation $r$ on $Q$, define the lifted relation
$\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$ on the tagged hierarchy $\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$ by
well-founded recursion on the child relation.  For atoms $q,q'\in Q$,
\[
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(\mathsf{atom}(q),\mathsf{atom}(q'))
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r(q,q').
\]
For a right node, an atom is related to it exactly when it is related to one
of its children.  A left node is related to an atom exactly when every one of
its children is related to that atom.  Finally, a left node is related to a
right node exactly when every left child is related to some right child.
The node may have any nonempty small index type; no branching restriction is
introduced.
\end{definition}
