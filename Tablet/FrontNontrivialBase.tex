\begin{helper}[Nontrivial front members and base]
Let $F$ be a front on $X$ and suppose $F\ne\{\emptyset\}$.  Every member
of $F$ is nonempty, and $\mathsf{FrontBase}(F)=X$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Assume first that $s\in F$ is empty.  Then $\emptyset\in F$.  For any
$t\in F$, if $t\ne\emptyset$, choose its least element $m$.  The finite
set $\{k\in t\mid k<m\}$ is empty, so the definition of
\noderef{InitialSegment} gives
$\mathsf{InitialSegment}(\emptyset,t)$.  Prefix-freeness from
\noderef{Front} then gives $t=\emptyset$.  Thus every member of $F$ is
empty, so $F=\{\emptyset\}$, contradicting the hypothesis.  Hence every
member is nonempty.

The base alternative in \noderef{Front} says either
$F=\{\emptyset\}$ or $\mathsf{FrontBase}(F)=X$.  The first alternative is
excluded by hypothesis, so the second holds.
\end{proof}
