\begin{lemma}[Stabilization under the shift]
For every $n,k$,
\[
\mathsf{PowerCopiedLeft}_r(h,\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x),n,k)
=\mathsf{PowerCopiedLeft}_r(h,x,n+1,k).
\]
Thus the copied triangle constructed from the shifted input is exactly the
next row of the triangle constructed from the original input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $k$.  At depth zero, use
\noderef{PowerShiftIterShift} to identify every shift iterate of
$\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x)$ with the next iterate of $x$; the two applications of
$\mathsf{PowerIResponse}$ from \noderef{PowerIResponse} therefore have identical arguments.  At a
successor depth, unfold \noderef{PowerCopiedLeft} and apply the induction
hypothesis to both recursive arguments, with row indices $n$ and $n+1$.
The deterministic response function then gives the required equality.
\end{proof}
