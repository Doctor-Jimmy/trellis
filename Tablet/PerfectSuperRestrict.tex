\begin{lemma}[Perfectness under sub-front restriction]
If a super-sequence is perfect, then its restriction to any sub-front is
perfect.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The finite-shift relation is unchanged by passing to a sub-front, and the
restricted value map is the original value map along the inclusion by
\noderef{SuperSequenceRestrict}.  The defining implication for
\noderef{PerfectSuperSequence} therefore restricts immediately.
\end{proof}
