\begin{lemma}[Support of the copied left values]
Under the hypotheses of \noderef{PowerCopiedFailureMove}, every selected
left value in row $n$ has support contained in the support of the row's
initial presentation:
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(L_{n,k})
\subseteq
\mathsf{PowerSupport}\bigl(h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x))\bigr).
\]
In particular, for the first row all copied values remain supported by
$h(x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a row $n$ and write
\[
L_{n,k}=\mathsf{PowerCopiedLeft}_r(h,x,n,k).
\]
We prove the displayed inclusion by induction on $k$.  At depth zero,
unfolding \noderef{PowerCopiedLeft} gives
\[
L_{n,0}=\mathsf{PowerIResponse}_r\bigl(
  h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x)),
  h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n+1,x))\bigr).
\]
The first assertion of \noderef{PowerCopiedFailureMove}, instantiated at
depth zero, says that the two values in this initial pair fail
$\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$.  Therefore \noderef{PowerIResponseLegal}, applied
to this failed pair and the displayed defining equation for $L_{n,0}$,
gives
\[
\mathsf{PowerMove}\bigl(h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x)),L_{n,0}\bigr).
\]
Applying \noderef{PowerMoveInSupport} yields
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(L_{n,0})
\subseteq
\mathsf{PowerSupport}\bigl(h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x))\bigr),
\]
which is the induction base.

For the successor step, assume
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(L_{n,k})
\subseteq
\mathsf{PowerSupport}\bigl(h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x))\bigr).
\]
The recursive clause in \noderef{PowerCopiedLeft} identifies
$L_{n,k+1}$ as the canonical response to the pair
$(L_{n,k},L_{n+1,k})$.  The second assertion of
\noderef{PowerCopiedFailureMove}, applied at depth $k$ in row $n$, gives
\[
\mathsf{PowerMove}(L_{n,k},L_{n,k+1}).
\]
Hence \noderef{PowerMoveInSupport} gives
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(L_{n,k+1})
\subseteq \mathsf{PowerSupport}(L_{n,k}).
\]
Composing this inclusion with the induction hypothesis proves the desired
inclusion at depth $k+1$.  Since $n$ was arbitrary, the result holds for
every row and depth.

Finally, when $n=0$, the zeroth-iterate clause of
\noderef{PowerShiftIter} gives $\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(0,x)=x$.
The displayed inclusion therefore specializes to support containment in
$\mathsf{PowerSupport}(h(x))$, as claimed.
\end{proof}
