\begin{lemma}[Support of the copied left values]
Under the hypotheses of \noderef{PowerCopiedFailureMove}, every selected
left value in row $n$ has support contained in the support of the row's
initial presentation:
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(L_{n,k})
\subseteq
\mathsf{PowerSupport}\bigl(h(\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,x))\bigr).
\]
In particular, for the first row all copied values remain supported by
$h(x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At depth zero, \noderef{PowerCopiedFailureMove} gives that $L_{n,0}$ is a
legal move from the initial value in row $n$.  The support inclusion
\noderef{PowerMoveInSupport} gives the claim.  At a successor depth, the
same lemma gives inclusion of the support of $L_{n,k+1}$ in that of
$L_{n,k}$; compose this with the induction hypothesis.  This proves the
inclusion for all depths and rows.
\end{proof}
