\begin{lemma}[Restriction preserves local constancy]
If $h$ is a locally constant multi-sequence and $z\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$,
then the restricted multi-sequence $h\mathbin{|}z$ is locally constant.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $x$.  A deciding prefix for $h$ at $z\circ x$ is supplied by
\noderef{LocallyConstant}.  Since $z$ is an order embedding, only finitely
many values of $x$ are needed to determine that finite prefix of $z\circ x$.
Choose a prefix of $x$ long enough to include those values.  Any $y$ with
that prefix has $z\circ y$ agreeing with $z\circ x$ on the deciding prefix,
so \noderef{MultiSequenceRestrict} gives
$(h\mathbin{|}z)(y)=(h\mathbin{|}z)(x)$.
\end{proof}
