\begin{lemma}[Perfect sub-super-sequences give perfect sub-multi-sequences]
Let $h$ be locally constant and let $h^{\dcl}$ be its induced
super-sequence on $F^h$.  If a restriction of $h^{\dcl}$ to a sub-front is
perfect, then $h$ has a perfect sub-multi-sequence.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate the infinite base of the sub-front increasingly.  Composing $h$
with this enumeration gives the required sub-multi-sequence.  For every
enumeration in that subbase, the front member selected by the deciding-front
construction lies in the sub-front, and the coherence equation in the
statement identifies its value with the value of $h$.  A shift of two such
members is a finite shift, so perfectness of the restricted super-sequence
gives the relation between consecutive values.  This is
\noderef{PerfectMultiSequence}.
\end{proof}
