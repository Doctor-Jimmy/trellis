\begin{lemma}[Perfect sub-super-sequences give perfect sub-multi-sequences]
Let $h$ be locally constant and let $h^{\dcl}$ be its induced
super-sequence on $F^h$.  If a restriction of $h^{\dcl}$ to a sub-front is
perfect, then $h$ has a perfect sub-multi-sequence.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $Y$ be the infinite base of the sub-front and enumerate it increasingly
by $z$.  The required restriction is the fixed-left restriction
$x\mapsto h(z\circ x)$, namely
$\mathsf{MultiSequenceRestrict}(h,z)$ by
\noderef{MultiSequenceRestrict}.  For every $x$, the deciding-front member
selected by the range of $z\circ x$ lies in the chosen sub-front; this is
the density of the sub-front together with the fact that it is a subfamily
of $F^h$.  The value equation for $h^{\dcl}$ therefore identifies the
restricted super-sequence value with $h(z\circ x)$.  The corresponding
member for $\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x)$ is related by a finite shift to the first
member, and perfectness of the restricted super-sequence gives the relation
between the two values.  Hence the fixed-left restriction is perfect in the
sense of \noderef{PerfectMultiSequence}.
\end{proof}
