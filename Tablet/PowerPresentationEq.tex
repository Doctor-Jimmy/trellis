\begin{definition}
The structural presentation equivalence
$\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}(X,Y)$ is defined recursively.  Two atoms are
equivalent exactly when their $Q$-values are equal.  An atom and a node are
never equivalent.  Two nodes are equivalent exactly when their children
match in both directions: every child of the first has an equivalent child
of the second, and every child of the second has an equivalent child of the
first.  This is an extensional equivalence of presentations, not mutual
relatedness for any later quasi-order.
\end{definition}
