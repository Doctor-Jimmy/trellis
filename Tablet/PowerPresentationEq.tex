\begin{definition}
The recursive relation $\mathsf{PowerPresentationEq}(X,Y)$ is defined by
matching the tagged presentations.  Two atoms match exactly when their
$Q$-values are equal.  An atom and a node never match.  Two nodes match
exactly when their children match in both directions: every child of the
first has a matching child of the second, and every child of the second has
a matching child of the first.
\end{definition}
