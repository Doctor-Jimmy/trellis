\begin{lemma}[Node--atom reduction]
If $X=\mathsf{node}(\iota,h,f)$ and $q\in Q$, then
\[
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(X,\mathsf{atom}(q))
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall i\in\iota\;\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(f(i),\mathsf{atom}(q)).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the well-founded recursion equation to the node--atom pair in the
definition \noderef{PowerRel}.  Its node--atom branch is precisely the
universal quantification over the children displayed above, and each term
under that quantifier is the corresponding recursive comparison.  This
gives the claimed equivalence.
\end{proof}
