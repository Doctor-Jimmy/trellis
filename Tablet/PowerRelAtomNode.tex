\begin{lemma}[Atom--node reduction]
If $q\in Q$ and $Y=\mathsf{node}(\iota,h,g)$, then
\[
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(\mathsf{atom}(q),Y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists i\in\iota\;\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(\mathsf{atom}(q),g(i)).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unfold \noderef{PowerRel} and apply its well-founded recursion equation at
the pair $(\mathsf{atom}(q),\mathsf{node}(\iota,h,g))$.  The atom--node
branch is defined to be the displayed existential, with the recursive call
made on the child pair by the lexicographic child relation.  Thus the two
propositions are definitionally the same after the recursion equation is
applied.
\end{proof}
