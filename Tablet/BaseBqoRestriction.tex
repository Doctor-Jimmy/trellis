\begin{lemma}[Base invariance of the bqo restriction property]
Assume that $r$ is a quasi-order and $Q$ is bqo.  For every infinite base
$X$ and every locally constant base multi-sequence on $X$, there is an
increasing embedding $z$ such that the restriction along $z$ is perfect.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the increasing enumeration of $X$ by
\noderef{InfiniteSetEnumeration}, and pull the base multi-sequence
back along it to a locally constant multi-sequence on
$\mathsf{IncSeq}$.  Local constancy is preserved because finite prefixes are
preserved by composition with an increasing embedding.  Apply the bqo
definition \noderef{Bqo} and the shift dichotomy \noderef{ShiftDichotomy}:
the bad alternative is impossible, so a perfect restriction exists.  Push
the resulting restriction forward along the enumeration of $X$.  The
composition identities in \noderef{BaseRightComp} and
\noderef{BaseRestrict} give the asserted base restriction.
\end{proof}
