\begin{lemma}[The prescribed left move is legal]
If $\mathsf{FrontBridge}(F,s,t,u)$ holds and the next left node $s'$ is
chosen by
\[
 (s\in F\text{ and }s'=s)\quad\text{or}\quad
 (s\notin F\text{ and }s'=u),
\]
then $s'\in T(F)$.  Moreover, in the first case $s'=s$, while in the
second case $s$ is a proper initial segment of $s'$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unpack the bridge clauses from \noderef{FrontBridge}.  In the first
case, the bridge already says $s\in T(F)$, and the equality $s'=s$
gives the conclusion.  In the second case, the bridge says both that
$u\in T(F)$ and that $s$ is a proper initial segment of $u$; substituting
$s'=u$ gives all three asserted facts.
\end{proof}
