\begin{definition}
The support $\mathsf{PowerSupport}(X)\subseteq Q$ is defined by induction
on the presentation.  An atom $q$ has support $\{q\}$, and the support of
a node is the union of the supports of all of its children.  This is the
recursive version of the paper's transitive-closure support and records
exactly the atoms occurring below a presentation.
\end{definition}
