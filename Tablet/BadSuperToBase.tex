\begin{lemma}[Super-sequence badness gives base badness]
If $f:F\to Q$ is a bad super-sequence on a front on $X$, then its extension
$f^{\upcl}$ from \noderef{SuperSequenceExtension} is a bad base
multi-sequence on $X$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The extension is locally constant by \noderef{SuperSequenceExtension}.
For $x\in\mathsf{BaseIncSeq}(X)$, choose the unique front members $s,t$
which prefix $x$ and its shift.  These members satisfy
$s\mathrel{\mathsf{FiniteShift}}t$, witnessed by $x$.  Badness of $f$ and
the extension value equations give
\(\neg r(f^{\upcl}(x),f^{\upcl}(\mathsf{BaseShift}_X(x)))\).
Thus the extension is bad in the sense of \noderef{BaseBad}.
\end{proof}
