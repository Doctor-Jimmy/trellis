\begin{lemma}[Badness under restriction]
If a multi-sequence $h$ is bad, then every restriction
$h\mathbin{|}z$ is bad.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At $x$, badness of $h$ is applied to the enumeration $x\circ z$.  Its
shift is $(x\circ z)\circ\mathsf{SuccSeq}$, which is the same enumeration
appearing in $(h\mathbin{|}z)(\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x))$ by
\noderef{RestrictionShift}.  Thus the required relation fails.
\end{proof}
