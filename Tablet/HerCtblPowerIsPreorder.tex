\begin{lemma}[The hereditary countable carrier is a preorder]
If $r$ is a preorder on $Q$, then
$\mathsf{HerCtblRel}(r)$ is a preorder on
$\mathsf{HerCtblPower}(Q)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The carrier is the subtype specified by
$\noderef{HerCtblPower}, and
$\mathsf{HerCtblRel}(r)$ is defined by the restriction in
$\noderef{HerCtblRel}$, applying $\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$ to
the underlying presentations.  Reflexivity follows from
$\noderef{PowerRelRefl}$ for each underlying presentation.  If
$\mathsf{HerCtblRel}(r)(x,y)$ and
$\mathsf{HerCtblRel}(r)(y,z)$, their underlying statements are the two
corresponding $\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$ relations, so transitivity follows from
$\noderef{PowerRelTrans}$.  The hereditary witnesses are carried by the
subtype and require no additional closure argument.
\end{proof}
