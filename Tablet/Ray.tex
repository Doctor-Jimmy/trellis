\begin{definition}
For a family $F$ of finite sets and $n\in\mathbb N$, its ray at $n$ is
\[
F_n=\{s\subseteq\mathbb N\text{ finite}\mid
       (\forall k\in s\; n<k)\text{ and }\{n\}\cup s\in F\}.
\]
When $n$ is in the base of a nontrivial front, this is the paper's family of
finite subsets of $X/n$ that follows $n$ in the front.
\end{definition}
