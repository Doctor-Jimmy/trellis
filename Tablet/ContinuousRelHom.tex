\begin{definition}
Let $s:\mathsf{IncSeq}\to\mathsf{IncSeq}$ and let $R$ be a binary
relation on a discrete type $A$.  A continuous homomorphism from
$(\mathsf{IncSeq},s)$ to $(A,R)$ is a map $H:\mathsf{IncSeq}\to A$ which is
locally constant in the finite-prefix sense and satisfies
\[
R\bigl(H(x),H(s(x))\bigr)
\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathsf{IncSeq}.
\]
\end{definition}
