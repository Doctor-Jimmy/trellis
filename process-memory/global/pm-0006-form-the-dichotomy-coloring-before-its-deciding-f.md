The perfect-or-bad shift dichotomy at paper lines 1617-1625 is obtained from the Boolean locally constant map `c_h(x)` recording whether `r (h x) (h (ShiftMap x))`, followed by the minimal deciding front of `c_h` and Nash-Williams homogeneity. Coloring the deciding front of `h` by comparing its value with an unspecified finite shift is not justified: a member of `F^h` does not canonically determine the shifted member or its value. Any formalization of `ShiftDichotomy` must build the Boolean map first, prove its local constancy, and apply the deciding-front construction to that map.
