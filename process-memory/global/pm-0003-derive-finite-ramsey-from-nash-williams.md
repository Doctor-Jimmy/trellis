Do not build a separate infinite Ramsey development for the Rado theorem. Once `cor:NashWill` is proved, apply it iteratively to the constant-length fronts of triples and quadruples to obtain exactly the homogeneous thinning used at paper lines 988-991. This places the Rado embedding target downstream of Nash-Williams and avoids duplicate combinatorics.
