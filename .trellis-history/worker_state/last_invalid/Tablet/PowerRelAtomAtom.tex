\begin{lemma}[Atom--atom reduction]
For $q,q'\in Q$,
\[
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(\mathsf{atom}(q),\mathsf{atom}(q'))
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r(q,q').
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unfold the well-founded recursive definition in \noderef{PowerRel} and
apply the defining equation of well-founded recursion at the pair of atoms.
The atom--atom branch of that definition is exactly the proposition
$r(q,q')$, so the displayed equivalence follows by reflexivity.
\end{proof}
