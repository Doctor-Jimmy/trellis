\begin{definition}
A relation $r$ on $Q$ is a better-quasi-order relation if there is no bad
locally constant multi-sequence into $Q$: for every locally constant
$h:\mathsf{IncSeq}\to Q$, the relation $r$ is not bad on $h$.
When $r$ is a quasi-order, this is the paper's definition of $Q$ being a
better-quasi-order.
\end{definition}
