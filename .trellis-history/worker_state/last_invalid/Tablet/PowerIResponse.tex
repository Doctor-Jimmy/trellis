\begin{definition}
For a relation $r$ on $Q$, define the canonical Player I response
$\mathsf{PowerIResponse}_r(x,y)$ by choosing, when one exists, a legal move
$x'$ from $x$ such that every legal response $y'$ from $y$ leaves the pair
$(x',y')$ outside $\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)$.  If no such witness exists, use
$x$ itself.  The choice is made once as a function of the two raw
presentations, so it is independent of any later input multi-sequence.
\end{definition}
