\begin{definition}[Bad sequence of sequences]
For a relation $r$ on $Q$, a map
$f:\mathsf{IncreasingPair}\to Q$ is bad when, for every $m<n<l$,
\[
\neg r\bigl(f(m,n),f(n,l)\bigr).
\]
Thus the map is the paper's sequence of sequences
$f:\,[\omega]^2\to Q$, and the condition is precisely its displayed
badness condition.
\end{definition}
