\begin{definition}
Let $f:F\to Q$ be a super-sequence on a front over $\mathbb N$, and let
$T(F)=\mathsf{PrefixTree}(F)$.  The map
$\widetilde f:T(F)\to\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$ is defined by well-founded recursion
along the proper-extension relation from
\noderef{FrontTreeWellFounded}.  At a front node $s$ it is the atom labelled
by $f(s)$.  At a non-front node $s$ it is the full hierarchy node whose
children are the values at all ordered one-point extensions
$s\cup\{n\}\in T(F)$.
\end{definition}
