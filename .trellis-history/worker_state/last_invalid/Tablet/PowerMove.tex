\begin{definition}
For presentations $x,x'\in\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$, write
$\mathsf{PowerMove}(x,x')$ when $x'$ is a legal move of Player I from $x$:
an atom must be left unchanged, while a node may be replaced by one of its
children.  Thus this is exactly the one-coordinate move relation in the
finite comparison game of the paper.
\end{definition}
