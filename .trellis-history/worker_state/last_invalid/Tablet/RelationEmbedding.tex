\begin{definition}[Relation embedding]
Given relations $r$ on $A$ and $s$ on $B$, a map $e:A\to B$ is a relation
embedding when it is injective and, for every $a,b\in A$,
\[
r(a,b)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad s(e(a),e(b)).
\]
It therefore preserves and reflects the relation, as required for the
embedding of Rado's order.
\end{definition}
