\begin{lemma}[Support decreases along a child]
If $\mathsf{PowerChild}(x,y)$, then
\[
\mathsf{PowerSupport}(x)\subseteq\mathsf{PowerSupport}(y).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unfold the child relation from \noderef{PowerChild}.  The right-hand
presentation must be a node and the left-hand presentation is one of its
children.  The recursive support definition in \noderef{PowerSupport} says
that the support of every child is one of the sets in the union defining the
support of the parent.  Thus every atom occurring below the child also occurs
below the parent, which is the required inclusion.
\end{proof}
