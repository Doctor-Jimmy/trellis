\begin{lemma}[Restriction and shift]
For every $z,x\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$,
\[
z\circ\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x)
=\mathsf{ShiftMap}(z\circ x).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{ShiftMap}, the left side is
$z\circ(x\circ\mathsf{SuccSeq})$.  The right side is
$(z\circ x)\circ\mathsf{SuccSeq}$.  These are equal by associativity of
composition, with the order recorded by \noderef{IncSeqComp}.  Applying
any multi-sequence to this equality gives the corresponding evaluation
identity for \noderef{MultiSequenceRestrict}.
\end{proof}
