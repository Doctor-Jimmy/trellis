\begin{definition}
For presentations $x,y\in\mathsf{PowerQ}(Q)$, write
$\mathsf{PowerChild}(x,y)$ when $y$ is a node and $x$ is one of the
children displayed by its indexing family.  Atoms have no children.  The
orientation is child-to-parent: this is the relation that decreases when a
recursive comparison moves down the hierarchy.
\end{definition}
