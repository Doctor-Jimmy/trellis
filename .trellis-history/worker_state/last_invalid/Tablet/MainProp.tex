\begin{theorem}[MainProp]
For every increasing injective function $g:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ with
$g\neq\mathrm{id}_{\mathbb N}$, the dynamical systems of increasing
embeddings given by right composition with $g$ and by the ordinary shift
are continuously homomorphically equivalent:
\[
(\mathsf{IncSeq},\mathsf{RightComp}(g))
\quad\text{and}\quad
(\mathsf{IncSeq},\mathsf{ShiftMap}).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The node \noderef{RmapLemma} supplies
\(\mathsf{ContMor}(\mathsf{RightComp}(g),\mathsf{ShiftMap})\), namely a
prefix-continuous homomorphism from right composition by $g$ to the shift.
The node \noderef{EmapLemma} supplies
\(\mathsf{ContMor}(\mathsf{ShiftMap},\mathsf{RightComp}(g))\), namely a
prefix-continuous homomorphism in the reverse direction.  By
\noderef{ContMorEq}, continuous homomorphic equivalence is exactly the
conjunction of these two statements.  Hence the two supplied homomorphisms
establish that the displayed systems are continuously homomorphically
equivalent.
\end{proof}
