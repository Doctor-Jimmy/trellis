\begin{lemma}[Shift iterates commute with the first shift]
For every $n$ and increasing enumeration $x$,
\[
\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n,\mathsf{ShiftMap}(x))
=\mathsf{PowerShiftIter}(n+1,x).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $n$.  The case $n=0$ is the defining equation for the zeroth
iterate.  In the successor case, unfold the recursive definition in
\noderef{PowerShiftIter} and apply the induction hypothesis under
$\mathsf{ShiftMap}$.  This proves the equality for every iterate.
\end{proof}
