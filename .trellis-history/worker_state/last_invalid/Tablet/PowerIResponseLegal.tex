\begin{lemma}[The canonical response is legal]
If $\neg\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(x,y)$, then
\[
\mathsf{PowerMove}\bigl(x,\mathsf{PowerIResponse}_r(x,y)\bigr).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The witness lemma \noderef{PowerIResponseWitness} supplies a move with the
required universal failure property.  Therefore the defining conditional in
\noderef{PowerIResponse} takes its positive branch, and its chosen value is
one of those witnesses.  The first conjunct of the witness is precisely the
assertion in the present lemma.
\end{proof}
