\begin{lemma}[The canonical response preserves failure]
If $\neg\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(x,y)$ and $y'$ is a legal move from $y$, then
\[
\neg\mathsf{PowerRel}\bigl(r,\mathsf{PowerIResponse}_r(x,y),y'\bigr).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{PowerIResponseWitness}, a witness with the universal failure
property exists.  The definition in \noderef{PowerIResponse}, together with
the positive branch justified by that witness, identifies the canonical
response with such a witness.  Applying its universal clause to the given
legal move \noderef{PowerMove} yields the conclusion.
\end{proof}
