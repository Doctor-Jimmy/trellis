\begin{definition}
For a multi-sequence $h:\mathsf{IncSeq}\to E$ and $z\in\mathsf{IncSeq}$,
define its sub-multi-sequence by
\[
(h\mathbin{|}z)(x)=h(z\circ x).
\]
Here $z$ is fixed on the left and $x$ varies on the right.  Thus the range
of $z\circ x$ is contained in the fixed range of $z$, so this is precisely
restriction to the infinite subbase enumerated by $z$; it is not the right
composition operation $x\mapsto x\circ z$.
\end{definition}
