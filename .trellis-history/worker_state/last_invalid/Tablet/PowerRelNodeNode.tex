\begin{lemma}[Node--node reduction]
For nodes $X=\mathsf{node}(\iota,h,f)$ and
$Y=\mathsf{node}(\kappa,h',g)$,
\[
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(X,Y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall i\in\iota\;\exists j\in\kappa\;
\mathsf{PowerRel}(r)(f(i),g(j)).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the well-founded recursion equation in \noderef{PowerRel} at the pair
of nodes.  The node--node branch first universally chooses a left index and
then existentially chooses a right index, and its recursive proposition is
exactly the comparison of the two selected children.  This is the displayed
equivalence.
\end{proof}
